## 1. Classic and emerging routes: durations, seasons, driving times, logistics pitfalls

### Takeaway
Turkey's regional circuits each come with hard seasonal limits that the planner must encode as rules:
- Kapadokya balloons fly on only about 220 days a year, and multi-day wind or fog cancellations happen even in May.
- Nemrut closes roughly December to mid-April because of snow.
- GAP sites hit about 40 °C in summer, so visit early in the morning.
- Rize and the Doğu Karadeniz are wet all year, and July carries the highest flood risk.
- The Likya Yolu is a spring or autumn trek, about 540 km over 25–45 days if walked whole.
- Doğu Ekspresi is a winter product that sells out. For 2026–27 it runs from 21 December 2026 to 12 March 2027.

Long driving legs make full-day drives a poor trade. Istanbul–Kapadokya is about 9 h by car against about 1 h 15 min by air, and planners advise keeping driving days under about 4 h.

### Cited Findings

**Driving distances/times (for the routing engine)**
- Istanbul → Cappadocia: about 9 h by road versus about 1 h 15 min by flight. The guide recommends flying. A forum traveller who drove it said it took a whole day, with little to see on the way. — [MyTurkeyAdventure road-trip planner](https://myturkeyadventure.com/turkey-road-trip-planner); [Fodor's forum](https://www.fodors.com/community/europe/turkey-11-day-trip.cfm?30)
- Antalya → Ürgüp (Cappadocia): about 615 km, about 8.5 h. Istanbul → Antalya: 725 km, about 11 h (likely a bus-time figure). — [TurkeyTravelPlanner, Antalya distances](https://turkeytravelplanner.com/go/med/Antalya/trans/distances.html)
- Antalya → Pamukkale: about 240 km, 3.5–4 h. Antalya → Fethiye on the coastal road: about 295 km, about 6 h. Antalya → Kaş: 185–190 km, about 3–3 h 15 min. Fethiye → Kaş: about 110 km, about 1 h 50 min. — [TurkeyTravelPlanner, Antalya](https://turkeytravelplanner.com/go/med/Antalya/trans/distances.html); [TurkeyTravelPlanner, Pamukkale](https://turkeytravelplanner.com/go/Aegean/Pamukkale/trans/distances_times.html)
- Pamukkale → Selçuk (Ephesus): about 190 km, about 3 h. A suggested stop on the way is Afrodisias. — [TurkeyTravelPlanner, Pamukkale](https://turkeytravelplanner.com/go/Aegean/Pamukkale/trans/distances_times.html)
- Planner rule from a road-trip guide: keep driving days under about 4 hours and break them with a site visit. Start the road trip after the Istanbul days, because city-centre congestion is heavy. — [MyTurkeyAdventure road-trip planner](https://myturkeyadventure.com/turkey-road-trip-planner); [Brendan's Adventures](https://brendansadventures.com/a-complete-guide-to-a-road-trip-in-turkey/)
- An overnight bus from Cappadocia to Antalya takes about 9 h. — [Fodor's forum](https://www.fodors.com/community/europe/turkey-draft-itinerary-1051599-printthread)

**Kapadokya (balloons, the main pitfall)**
- Hot-air balloon flights are possible on about 220 days per year on average. The SHGM Nevşehir coordination office decides each morning, and balloons may not lift off when wind limits are exceeded. — reported in [TRT Haber, Jan 2026](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/kapadokyada-balon-turlari-4-gundur-yapilamiyor-932870.html); [Evrensel, 19 May 2026](https://www.evrensel.net/haber/5984713/kapadokyada-balon-turlari-olumsuz-hava-kosullari-nedeniyle-yapilmadi)
- January 2026: flights had been cancelled for 4 consecutive days from 26 January because of wind, precipitation and fog. — [TRT Haber](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/kapadokyada-balon-turlari-4-gundur-yapilamiyor-932870.html)
- May 2026: no flights from 17 May, and the 19 May flag flight was cancelled. Shoulder season is not safe either. — [Evrensel, 19 May 2026](https://www.evrensel.net/haber/5984713/kapadokyada-balon-turlari-olumsuz-hava-kosullari-nedeniyle-yapilmadi); [Gazete Oksijen](https://gazeteoksijen.com/turkiye/kapadokyada-19-mayis-icin-yapilacak-turk-bayrakli-ucus-iptal-edildi-276031)
- Prices and the seasons that drive them are covered in section 4. TÜRSAB says demand and prices rise from May to mid-October and are much lower from November to February. — [Yeni Birlik](https://www.gazetebirlik.com/yerel-haberler/kapadokyadaki-balon-turu-fiyatlari-dudak-ucuklatiyor/126982)
- Where to stay in Cappadocia: Göreme has the highest demand (balloon views, closest to the valleys), Uçhisar is mostly upper-segment, Ürgüp is the balanced-price alternative, and Avanos is cheaper. — [Setur, Kapadokya mağara otelleri](https://www.setur.com.tr/kapadokya-magara-otelleri)

**Ege (İzmir–Urla–Çeşme–Alaçatı–Şirince–Efes–Pamukkale–Bodrum)**
- Urla Bağ Yolu sits about 1 h from İzmir: stone houses, olive groves, vineyards. — [Turna blog](https://www.turna.com/blog/?p=25064)
- Its founding year is reported as 2013 in an academic paper and as 2016 in Paradergi; the 2016 date may be a later project phase. Named wineries include Şatomet, Urlice, USCA, Mozaik and Urla Şarapları, and Ayda in Gödence is just outside the route. Chef restaurants include Od Urla, Manej, Urla Vino Locale, Ma Urla and Teruar. — [OGÜ Avesis paper](https://avesis.ogu.edu.tr/yayin/058609f6-f24d-4d93-98af-6f67745b3101/destinasyon-cekicilik-unsuru-olarak-bag-rotalari-urla-bag-yolu-rotasindaki-sarap-evlerine-yonelik-cevrimici-yorumlarin-degerlendirmesi); [Paradergi, Oct 2023](https://paradergi.com.tr/life-style/2023/10/03/bag-bozumu-deneyimi-icin-turkiyeden-adresler); [Hürriyet, Ebru Erke](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/yazarlar/ebru-erke/baglarin-arasinda-lezzet-yolculugu-42146633)
- Çeşme/Alaçatı: even the cheapest hotels cost more than in other coastal regions, and most hotels sell room plus breakfast (oda-kahvaltı) rather than all-inclusive. The article is undated. — [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/ekonomi/otellerde-bayram-tarifesi-10-bin-tlden-basliyor-500-bin-tlyi-asiyor-42285879)
- Bodrum 2026 season:
  - Hotel room prices rose about 20% year on year.
  - Bodrum has only 14 luxury hotels against about 860 mid- and low-segment properties. The Bodrum hoteliers' association says to use median rather than mean prices.
  - A separate, undated report puts room prices at 2,000–43,000 TL per person per night. — [CNBC-e](https://www.cnbce.com/haberler/otelciler-sezonu-cnbc-eye-degerlendirdi-bodrumda-fiyatlar-yuzde-20-artti-h33287); [Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/bodrum-da-konaklama-43-bin-plaj-ucreti-ise-6-bin-tl-ye-kadar-cikiyor-3792745-ekonomi)
- Summer night museum hours (1 June–1 October) cover Efes until 00:00, and Hierapolis (Pamukkale), Aspendos, Patara and Side until 23:00. In summer, schedule these Ege and Akdeniz sites for the evening. — [DHA](https://www.dha.com.tr/kultur-sanat/20-muze-ve-oren-yeri-gece-ziyaretine-acildi-2883050); [Dünya](https://www.dunya.com/kultur-sanat/gece-muzeciligi-basliyor-27-noktada-muzeler-artik-geceleri-de-acik-haberi-778726)

**Akdeniz / Likya Yolu**
- Length: about 540 km, with sources ranging from 505 to 555 km. The route runs from Ovacık village near Fethiye (Muğla) to Geyikbayırı in Konyaaltı (Antalya). Walking it whole takes 25–45 days (Time Out says about 30) and it is usually split into sections. — [Enuygun, 5 soruda Likya Yolu](https://www.enuygun.com/bilgi/5-soruda-likya-yolu/); [Habertürk (Time Out ranking)](https://www.haberturk.com/likya-yolu-dunyanin-en-guzel-yuruyus-rotasi-secildi-3834539); [Etstur](https://www.etstur.com/letsgo/likya-yolu-tarihi-yuruyus-rehberi/)
- Best seasons are autumn (September–October) and spring (March–April). Time Out gives September–November. — [Enuygun](https://www.enuygun.com/bilgi/5-soruda-likya-yolu/); [Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/likya-yolu-dunyanin-en-guzel-yuruyus-rotasi-secildi-3834539)
- Example stage: stage 1 is 65 km at about 13 km per day and is the easiest section. — [Milliyet](https://milliyet.com.tr/galeri/sarp-ozkarin-gozunden-tarihi-likya-yolu-1-etap-6308813)
- Lycian-coast site passes for foreigners, from the 2027 tariff: Olympos PassCard €65, Patara PassCard €60 (10 beach entries), Phaselis PassCard €65. Museum Pass Akdeniz is €100 for 7 days and covers Antalya, Mersin, Adana and Denizli. These prices take effect 1 April 2027. — [KTB tariff PDF via Alomaliye, Sept 2026](https://www.alomaliye.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2027-muze-orenyeri-yabanci.pdf)

**Karadeniz (Trabzon–Uzungöl–Rize–Ayder–Artvin, yaylalar)**
- Rize is Turkey's wettest province. MGM measured 1,710 mm in 2020, 1,630 mm in the 2017–18 water year and 1,289 mm in 2015; media often repeat a figure of about 2,400 kg/m². The Karadeniz region has 175 or more rainy days a year. — [MGM 2020](https://mgm.gov.tr/FILES/arastirma/yagis-degerlendirme/2020alansal.pdf); [MGM 2017–18](https://www.mgm.gov.tr/FILES/arastirma/yagis-degerlendirme/2017-2018AlansalYagisDegerlendirmesi.pdf); [BirGün](https://www.birgun.net/haber/turkiye-nin-en-cok-yagis-alan-ili-rize-de-su-sikintisi-319747)
- July is the month with the highest disaster (flood/landslide) value in Rize. One July episode brought 80 consecutive days of rain. — [Türkgün](https://www.turkgun.com/gundem/afet-degeri-en-yuksek-ay-temmuz/214664)
- Uzungöl lies at 1,090 m and is described as losing its natural character to rapid construction. Ayder is the most-visited yayla. — [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/seyahat/dogu-karadeniz-de-bu-yaz-12288897)
- Yayla şenlikleri run mainly in August. One example is the Düzköy Honofter Yaylası Şenliği on 20 August; that date comes from an older article and needs a yearly check. — [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/seyahat/dogu-karadeniz-de-bu-yaz-12288897)
- Autumn foliage trips are a thing, and Uzungöl bookings rise before school mid-term breaks (ara tatil). — [CNN Türk](https://www.cnnturk.com/turkiye/karadenizde-ara-tatil-oncesi-otel-ve-pansiyonlara-rezervasyonlar-artti-1721951)

**GAP (Gaziantep–Şanlıurfa–Göbeklitepe–Mardin–Nemrut)**
- Göbeklitepe:
  - Go early in the morning, because crowds rise sharply toward noon. Allow at least 2–3 hours, and expect strong summer sun on an open-air site.
  - Visitors have reported about 40 °C days. Şanlıurfa city sees around 45 °C, and the site can feel cooler than the city.
  - The only firm hours found are 08:00–16:30, seven days a week, from 2018.
  - Sources: [Pegasus blog](https://www.flypgs.com/blog/?p=8085); [Urfanatik](https://www.urfanatik.com/video/45-derecelik-sanliurfa-sicaginda-gobeklitepeden-serin-mola); [Hürriyet 2018](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/kelebek/keyif/gobeklitepe-tum-gun-ziyarete-acik-olacak-41022406)
- Göbeklitepe crowds: about 10,000 visitors a day during the school mid-term break. A July 2026 headline speaks of a target of 2 million tourists. A second excavation area (GT2, under a green roof) was announced as opening to visitors; its opening date is unverified. — [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/n-life/gezi/gobeklitepe-ara-tatilde-gunluk-10-bin-civarinda-ziyaretci-agirliyor,PlymHm8PtUyaNdS7AohwAg); [Karar, 31 Jul 2026](https://www.karar.com/gobeklitepede-hedef-2-milyon-turist-1509055); [CNN Türk](https://www.cnnturk.com/turkiye/gobeklitepede-yeni-kazi-alani-ziyarete-aciliyor-2351118)
- Nemrut Dağı:
  - The tourism season closes roughly December–March because of snow. It opens once the road is cleared, usually mid-April.
  - In April 2026 the first groups met a snow-covered 2,206 m summit, with 1–2 m of snow on the west terrace.
  - The summit terraces are reached by about a 1 km walk. Sunrise and sunset are the draws.
  - In summer night-museum mode Nemrut admits visitors from 04:00 for sunrise.
  - Sources: [Medyascope, 21 Apr 2026](https://medyascope.tv/2026/04/21/nemrut-sezonun-ilk-turistlerini-karla-karsiladi/); [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/seyahat/galeri-nemrut-dagi-sezonun-son-turistlerini-agirliyor,wDpEWKHCIUGg0_Msm_Ldjw); [İstiklal](https://www.istiklal.com.tr/kultur-sanat/dunya-mirasi-nemrut-sezonu-acti-442034h); [DHA](https://www.dha.com.tr/kultur-sanat/20-muze-ve-oren-yeri-gece-ziyaretine-acildi-2883050)
- Hatay/Antakya after the February 2023 earthquakes:
  - Before the quakes the historic Uzun Çarşı had more than 2,500 businesses, and more than 600 were destroyed.
  - As of February 2026, 1,102 shops were being rebuilt, with staged handover to shopkeepers from April 2026.
  - Meanwhile a temporary prefab bazaar of 374 shops operates in Haraparası Mahallesi.
  - Sources: [TRT Haber, Feb 2026](https://www.trthaber.com/haber/turkiye/antakyanin-tarihi-carsisi-ayaga-kalkiyor-933482.html); [DHA](https://www.dha.com.tr/foto-galeri/600-dukkanin-yikildigi-tarihi-uzun-carsida-hayata-donus-cabasi-2233352)

**Doğu (Kars, Ani, Doğu Ekspresi)**
- Turistik Doğu Ekspresi 2026–27: 70 services in total.
  - Ankara → Kars: first train Monday 21 December 2026, then Monday, Wednesday and Friday until 10 March 2027.
  - Kars → Ankara: 23 December 2026 to 12 March 2027.
  - Individual ticket sales are expected to open 26 October 2026 through TCDD Taşımacılık.
  - The trip takes about 30 hours.
  - Sources: [Ensonhaber](https://www.ensonhaber.com/dogu-ekspresi-2026-2027-takvimi-aciklandi-iste-bilet-fiyatlari-ve-tarihler-h1572817); [Milli Gazete](https://www.milligazete.com.tr/dogu-ekspresi-bilet-fiyatlari-2026-2027-kac-tl-turistik-dogu-ekspresi-ne-zaman-baslayacak-bilet-nereden-alinir); [Jollytur](https://www.jollytur.com/turistik-dogu-ekspresi-ajet-ile-kars-turu-1-gece-otel-2-gece-tren-konaklamali-ankara-cikisli-kars-ankara-yonu)
- In winter, Doğu Ekspresi tickets and rental cars in Kars sell out fast. — [Yolcu360 Kars](https://yolcu360.com/lokasyonlar/turkiye/kars)
- Ani sits on the Armenian border and was once the region's largest, richest city; the Menuçehr Camii is one highlight. Çıldır Gölü offers sleigh and horse-sleigh rides only when the lake is frozen. Tour packages include Kars cheese tastings and the city's Russian/Baltic-era architecture. — [Jollytur itinerary](https://www.jollytur.com/turistik-dogu-ekspresi-ajet-ile-kars-turu-1-gece-otel-2-gece-tren-konaklamali-ankara-cikisli-kars-ankara-yonu); [Milliyet](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/gundem/kisin-bir-baska-guzel-6115698)

**Trakya wine route**
- Trakya Bağ Rotası, created around 2013 with a Trakya Kalkınma Ajansı grant, is described as Turkey's first wine route.
  - It links 12 boutique wineries in Tekirdağ, Kırklareli, Şarköy and Gelibolu.
  - Arcadia, Vino Dessera, Barbare and Porto Caeli offer lodging and a restaurant.
  - Barel, Suvla, Arda and Akın Gürbüz offer a restaurant only.
  - The source is older, so verify these are still operating.
  - Sources: [BirGün](https://www.birgun.net/makale/agro-turizm-baglar-gastronomi-ve-sarap-260264); [Paradergi](https://paradergi.com.tr/life-style/2023/10/03/bag-bozumu-deneyimi-icin-turkiyeden-adresler)
- Passing Çanakkale or Gelibolu on a Trakya–Ege route means paying the 1915 Çanakkale Köprüsü toll: 1,170 TL by car from 1 July 2026 (see section 4).

**Frig Vadisi**
- The valley spans Afyonkarahisar, Eskişehir and Kütahya. Highlights are the Ayazini rock houses, the Göynüş Vadisi monuments, the Midas Anıtı at Yazılıkaya and the Frig Yolu trails.
- Afyon suits first-timers, Yazılıkaya suits history fans, and the Kütahya side suits quiet hikers.
- The Yazılıkaya site (Han, Eskişehir) is listed as open 08:00–17:00. Sample hikes run 6–10 km over 3–5 h. Spring and autumn are recommended.
- Sources: [Pegasus blog, Frig Vadisi](https://www.flypgs.com/blog/frig-vadisi-gezi-rehberi-gezilecek-yerler-ve-frig-yolu-rotalari/); [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/seyahat/galeri-turkiyenin-yeni-turizm-markasi-frigya-vadileri-olacak,TZrcImpiW02k1v1q8KED7w)

**Istanbul**
- Topkapı is closed on Tuesdays. Aim for the 09:00 opening at Topkapı and the Archaeology Museums. — [istanbul.com, Apr 2026](https://istanbul.com/blog/museums/istanbul-museum-pass); [Türkgün](https://www.turkgun.com/kultur-sanat/topkapi-sarayi-gece-giris-ucreti-2026-ne-kadar-topkapi-sarayi-gece-ziyaretinde-muze-kart-geciyor-mu/373472)
- Full hours and closing days for Istanbul museums are in section 3.

### Inferences
- **Weather-gated activities need a flex buffer.** Balloon flights are available on only about 220 days a year, with multi-day cancellation runs such as 26–29 January and 17–19 May 2026. Any Kapadokya plan that includes a balloon should therefore hold at least 2 sunrise slots, putting the balloon on the first morning and keeping the second as a fallback. The replan alternative for a cancellation is a sunrise viewpoint (watching balloons from the ground) followed by a valley hike.
- **Season masks per region (derived from the cited findings):**
  - Nemrut: closed about December to mid-April.
  - GAP: in June–September, put outdoor sites at 07:00–11:00 and use a midday indoor block (museums, bazaar, lunch).
  - Likya: walk March–April and September–November; avoid midsummer for long stages.
  - Doğu Ekspresi: late December to mid-March only.
  - Karadeniz: rain is possible on any day, so always attach an indoor or covered alternative. Flag July for landslide and flood risk on mountain roads.
- **Mode choice.** For legs above about 6–7 h of driving (Istanbul–Kapadokya, Istanbul–Antalya, Istanbul–GAP), the planner should default to suggesting a flight, YHT or overnight bus. A rental car suits the regional loops: Antalya–Kaş–Fethiye, Selçuk–Pamukkale–Bodrum, and the Kapadokya valleys.
- **Durations.** The following are model knowledge, not verified in this session, and should be treated as defaults to tune: Istanbul 3–4 days; Kapadokya 2–3 nights; Ege loop (İzmir/Urla/Çeşme + Efes/Şirince + Pamukkale) 5–7 days; Likya coast by car 4–6 days; Karadeniz 5–7 days; GAP (Antep–Urfa–Mardin–Nemrut) 5–7 days; Kars/Ani 2–3 days plus the train; Frig Vadisi 1–2 days; Safranbolu–Amasra 2–3 days.

### Gaps
- No sourced driving times were found for Karadeniz legs (Trabzon–Uzungöl, Rize–Ayder, Artvin), GAP legs (Antep–Urfa–Mardin–Kahta/Nemrut) or Safranbolu–Amasra. Use a routing API, such as OSRM or Google, or the KGM mesafe cetveli instead of static numbers.
- Safranbolu–Amasra: no sources were retrieved. Season, hours and logistics are unverified.
- No sourced 2026 data on Karadeniz yayla road conditions or minibus access, on Mardin and Şanlıurfa summer temperatures by month, or on Kars winter temperatures.
- No official 2026 summary of balloon flight-day counts or cancellation rates. The 754,098-passenger statistic found ([Bengütürk](https://www.benguturk.com/gundem/kapadokyada-balon-turlarina-754-bin-98-kisi-katildi-259819h)) is undated.
- Current Göbeklitepe opening hours and whether GT2 has opened are unverified.

## 2. Local experience categories a "tour guide" should cover

### Takeaway
The planner's content model needs these category types, each with region-specific "signature" items: kahvaltı (serpme or köy), esnaf lokantası, kebapçı/ocakbaşı, meyhane, pide/lahmacun, çay bahçesi/kahvehane, tarihi çarşı, semt pazarı, coğrafi işaretli ürün shopping, and wine route.

Turkey had about 1,881 registered coğrafi işaretli ürün (1,842 as of March 2026), with Gaziantep the leading province. Gaziantep, Hatay and Afyonkarahisar are UNESCO Creative Cities of Gastronomy. These give a sourced backbone for "what to eat or buy here".

### Cited Findings

**Breakfast**
- Serpme kahvaltı in Istanbul: Van Kahvaltı Evi in Cihangir costs about 450–600 TL per person for the full spread (April 2026). Specialties include otlu Van peyniri and murtuğa. Tea is included at some breakfast spreads and charged separately at others. — [istanbul.com, Best Turkish breakfast (dated 28 Jun 2026, prices Apr 2026)](https://istanbul.com/blog/food-drink/best-turkish-breakfast-istanbul)

**Esnaf lokantası and "where locals eat"**
- Esnaf lokantası menus rotate homestyle dishes for local workers and office staff. Examples:
  - Ata Lokantası, open since the late 1980s.
  - Mahir Lokantası in Osmanbey, with wood-oven daily regional specials.
  - Şahin Lokantası, with shared tables.
  - Mutfak Dili in Karaköy has closed, which is a reminder that venue data goes stale.
  - Source: [Culinary Backstreets, esnaf lokantası tag](https://culinarybackstreets.com/tag/esnaf%20lokantasi); [Culinary Backstreets Istanbul](https://culinarybackstreets.com/stories/istanbul)
- The "lunch hunting" method: walk into a small-commerce street, ask a shopkeeper (cobbler, hardware store) where they eat lunch, repeat the question clearly, and follow them to the nearest esnaf lokantası. — [Culinary Backstreets](https://culinarybackstreets.com/stories/istanbul)

**Regional specialties (sourced subset)**
- Kastamonu: Taşköprü sarımsağı, etli ekmek, kuyu kebabı, banduma, çekme helva, pastırma. A compilation recorded 812 dishes in Kastamonu. — [CNN Türk](https://www.cnnturk.com/seyahat/kastamonunun-neyi-meshur-kastamonunun-en-meshur-yemekleri-ve-alinabilecek-hediyelikler-2096545)

### Inferences
- **Data model.** Each venue should carry:
  - `category` (kahvaltıcı, esnaf_lokantası, kebapçı, ocakbaşı, meyhane, pideci, balıkçı, çay_bahçesi, kahvehane, tatlıcı/baklavacı, çarşı, semt_pazarı, şarap_evi)

  - `price_tier`
  - `weekly_pattern` (for example `market_day: Tue`)
  - `closed_on` (weekday, plus bayram day 1)
  - `source_last_verified`
  - `signature_items[]`, linked to coğrafi işaret IDs
- **Esnaf lokantası as the lunch default.** Esnaf lokantaları are lunch-centric, with rotating pots that run out by mid-afternoon. They are the right default for the "lunch here" slot for budget and authenticity personas. The meyhane and ocakbaşı slots belong to dinner.
- **Venue freshness.** The Mutfak Dili closure and the Antakya rebuild show venue data decays. Store a `last_verified` date per venue and down-rank stale ones.
- **Ingest the coğrafi işaret registry.** Ingest the TÜRKPATENT registry (about 1,881 products, mapped to province) so the "shop here" suggestion can say "buy X, a registered GI from this province".

## 3. Operating realities: hours, closing days, Müzekart vs foreigner pricing, mosques, Ramadan, bayram, markets, alcohol

### Takeaway
**Two-tier museum pricing.** Foreigners pay Euro-denominated prices charged in lira at the day's rate. Examples: Ayasofya mosque €25 (one source €27.5), Efes €40 plus Teras Evler €15, Galata €30, Topkapı about €55–70. Turkish citizens use a Müzekart, reported at 60 TL a year; that figure was confirmed in 2023 and is unconfirmed for 2026.

**Museum Pass for foreigners.**
- 2026 prices: Türkiye about €165, İstanbul about €105.
- From 1 April 2027: Türkiye €185, İstanbul €120, Ege €105, Akdeniz €100, Kapadokya €75.

**Calendar effects the planner must encode:**
- Weekly closures: Topkapı Tuesday; Archaeology Museums, Dolmabahçe and Rumeli Hisarı Monday; Kapalıçarşı Sunday.
- Mosques close to tourists during the five namaz times and Friday noon.
- Summer night museums run 1 June–1 October.
- Bayram periods: state motorways are often toll-free, but YİD bridges still charge, and transport sells out.
- Ramadan: traffic is chaotic before iftar.
- Retail alcohol is banned 22:00–06:00.

**Upcoming holidays:**
- 29 Ekim (Cumhuriyet Bayramı).
- 2027 Ramazan Bayramı 9–11 March (arife 8 March).
- 2027 Kurban Bayramı 16–19 May (arife 15 May).

### Inferences
- **Pricing by nationality.** The planner must ask the traveler's nationality and residency, plus whether they hold a Müzekart. Pricing differs by orders of magnitude: a Turkish family pays one 60 TL card per adult per year, while a foreign couple pays about €25–55 per site per person.
- **Price shape.** Store foreigner prices as EUR and convert with the day's TCMB rate. Store citizen prices as TL.
- **Expected 2026→2027 increases.** The 2027 Museum Pass prices rise about 12% for Türkiye (€165→€185) and about 14% for İstanbul (€105→€120). Expect similar EUR increases for single-site tickets from 1 April 2027 (inference from the tariff timing).
- **Hard constraints for the scheduler:**
  - `closed_weekday` per site: Topkapı Tuesday; Arkeoloji, Dolmabahçe and Rumeli Hisarı Monday; Kapalıçarşı Sunday.
  - `last_entry` usually about 1 h before closing.
  - Mosques: block namaz windows (compute daily from Diyanet prayer times) and Friday about 11:30–14:00.
  - Bayram day 1: bazaars closed.
  - Summer: allow evening slots for night-museum sites.
- **Ramadan 2027.** Ramadan 2027 should start about 8 February 2027, inferred from Ramazan Bayramı starting 9 March 2027. Inside that window the planner should:
  - flag iftar time;
  - avoid scheduling taxi transfers in the pre-iftar window;
  - suggest booking iftar dinners;
  - suggest Sultanahmet/Eyüp iftar atmosphere as an experience.
- **Alcohol rule.** The planner should never schedule a "buy wine at the market" step after 22:00.

## 5. Tourist traps and scams; "where locals eat" heuristics

### Takeaway
2026 sources agree on the main Istanbul traps:
- the shoeshine brush drop;
- taxis with no meter or a "broken" meter (use BiTaksi or Uber);
- the bar or nightclub bill scam on Beyoğlu/İstiklal targeting solo men;
- barber-shop add-on bills;
- carpet shops reached through friendly "guides";
- unofficial tour kiosks and suspiciously cheap balloon offers;
- bazaar exchange boards;
- the Kumkapı fish-strip hard sell.

The 30 January 2026 ban on servis, kuver and masa charges gives the planner a legal basis to warn users.

### Cited Findings
- **Shoeshine trick.** The shiner drops a brush; when you return it you get a "free" shine and then a demand for payment, often 1,000 TRY or more. Documented in Karaköy and Sultanahmet. Ignore the brush, or call out rather than pick it up. — [MyTurkeyAdventure, 2026](https://myturkeyadventure.com/blog/turkey-tour-scams-2026-real-warnings-from-a-local-operator); [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/travel-tips/istanbul-scams/)
- **Free rose or bracelet,** then a public scene demanding payment. **Fake tourist police** who "inspect" your cash. Offer to go to a Polis Merkezi instead. — [MyTurkeyAdventure](https://myturkeyadventure.com/blog/turkey-tour-scams-2026-real-warnings-from-a-local-operator)
- **Barber shops** in Sultanahmet, Taksim, İstiklal, Kaleiçi and cruise ports: a 350 TRY headline becomes a 5,000–10,000+ TRY bill. Card terminals may be pre-typed, and staff may block the exit. Get an itemised price in writing; the tourism police number is given as 155. — [MyTurkeyAdventure](https://myturkeyadventure.com/blog/turkey-tour-scams-2026-real-warnings-from-a-local-operator)
- **Taxis:** a "broken" meter followed by an inflated flat fare (for example 300 TRY quoted as 1,400–2,000), detours, and big-note/small-note swaps. Use BiTaksi or Uber, or official ranks with the meter on. Do not take the first airport tout; consider Havaist/Havabus. — [MyTurkeyAdventure](https://myturkeyadventure.com/blog/turkey-tour-scams-2026-real-warnings-from-a-local-operator); [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/travel-tips/istanbul-scams/)
- **Bar scam** in Beyoğlu/İstiklal: a friendly stranger leads you to an unnamed bar, hostesses join, and the bill is inflated. Reports range from €200–500 to thousands of dollars, with escorts to ATMs. — [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/travel-tips/istanbul-scams/); [MyTurkeyAdventure](https://myturkeyadventure.com/blog/turkey-tour-scams-2026-real-warnings-from-a-local-operator)
- **Carpet shops:** a "helpful guide" near Sultanahmet invites you for tea at a family shop, followed by hours of pressure and a commission for the guide. — [MyTurkeyAdventure](https://myturkeyadventure.com/blog/turkey-tour-scams-2026-real-warnings-from-a-local-operator)
- **Unofficial tour resellers** in Sultanahmet and Cappadocia:
  - Inflated dinner cruises and Efes day trips.
  - Balloon operators who overbook and then cancel citing "technical" or weather reasons.
  - Verify TÜRSAB licences.
  - Requests to pay by Western Union, MoneyGram or a personal account are a red flag.
  - Source: [MyTurkeyAdventure](https://myturkeyadventure.com/blog/turkey-tour-scams-2026-real-warnings-from-a-local-operator)
- **Currency exchange** in the Grand and Spice Bazaars: boards show rates valid only for amounts over $5,000, and staff may palm notes. Use bank ATMs and count your cash at the counter. **Hotel booking fraud:** fake listings and bait-and-switch. — [MyTurkeyAdventure](https://myturkeyadventure.com/blog/turkey-tour-scams-2026-real-warnings-from-a-local-operator)
- **Fish restaurants:** Culinary Backstreets skips the Kumkapı fish-restaurant strip because of aggressive hosts steering diners to overpriced places, and goes to a side-street lokanta (Doyuran) instead. — [Culinary Backstreets](https://culinarybackstreets.com/stories/istanbul)
- **Hidden charges are now illegal.** From 30 January 2026, servis, kuver and masa charges are banned and menu prices must be final. Fines are being issued. — [Alomaliye](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/01/30/restoranlarda-servis-kuver-ve-masa-ucreti-yasaklandi/); [Halk TV](https://halktv.com.tr/ekonomi/gizli-servis-ve-kuver-ucreti-gundem-olmustu-bakanliktan-aciklama-geldi-1006658h)
- **Overall risk:** Istanbul is broadly safe, and petty theft is the most common problem. A 24/7 tourism police unit operates mainly around Sultanahmet; a tourist hotline of 444 0 887 is quoted but unverified. — [The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/istanbul/is-istanbul-safe/amp/); [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/travel-tips/istanbul-scams/)
- **The local's method:** ask shopkeepers on small-commerce streets where they eat lunch. — [Culinary Backstreets](https://culinarybackstreets.com/stories/istanbul)

### Inferences
- **Geofenced warnings.** Attach `scam_warnings[]` to areas and show them when the traveler's plan enters:
  - Sultanahmet: carpet "guide", tour kiosks, shoeshine.
  - Karaköy: shoeshine.
  - Beyoğlu/İstiklal at night: bar scam.
  - Kumkapı: fish strip.
  - Kapalıçarşı/Mısır Çarşısı: FX boards.
  - Airports: taxi touts.
  - Göreme: cheap balloon offers.
- **"Where locals eat" heuristics.** These are derived from the sources above plus model knowledge, flagged as heuristics:
  - prefer venues one or two streets off the main tourist axis;
  - prefer menus with prices printed in TL, now legally final;
  - rotating daily pots (esnaf lokantası) and lunch crowds of workers are good signs;
  - avoid hosts who solicit on the street;
  - for fish priced by weight, ask the kilo price and the weight before cooking.
- **Balloon-booking check.** Flag balloon offers below about €150 in high season, and always show the operator's cancellation and refund policy.

## 6. Travel personas relevant to Turkey and how plans should differ

### Takeaway
Persona drives which pricing tier applies, which calendar matters, and which content the planner shows:
- **Price tier:** citizen Müzekart versus foreigner EUR tickets.
- **Calendar:**
  - Domestic families travel in bayram and ara tatil peaks.
  - Likya trekkers use spring or autumn windows.
  - Doğu Ekspresi travellers use the winter window.
- **Content priorities:**
  - Gastronomy travellers: GI products, UNESCO food cities, wine routes.
  - History buffs: Museum Pass economics, night museums, early-morning GAP sites.

### Cited Findings
- **Turkish domestic travellers:**
  - A Müzekart (reported at 60 TL a year) gives unlimited entry to 350+ sites. Under-18s and over-65s get free mobile cards.

### Inferences
- **Persona flags.** Inputs that switch rules:
  - `citizen_or_resident` (pricing tier, Müzekart);
  - `has_car`;
  - `kids_ages`;
  - `pace` (for example max sites per day, max driving hours);
  - `alcohol_ok`;
  - `diet` (vegetarian, vegan, halal-strict);
  - `budget_tier`;
  - `interests` (food, history, nature, beach, trekking, wine).
- **Domestic families.** Plan around school holidays and bayram. Pre-book transport and hotels and expect peak pricing. Prefer bayram-free days for Göbeklitepe, Uzungöl and Kapadokya. Suggest balloon-watching as a free alternative and lean on the Müzekart's value.
- **Foreign first-timers.** Classic triangle Istanbul → Kapadokya (fly) → Ege or Akdeniz. Run a Museum Pass break-even calculation, show scam warnings, recommend app taxis, and favour EUR display.
- **Couples.** Boutique cave hotels (Uçhisar, Ürgüp), Urla/Alaçatı wine and dining, sunset slots, meyhane dinners.
- **Solo backpackers.** Overnight buses, Göreme pensions, Kadıköy stays, esnaf lokantası lunches, and the solo-male bar-scam warning.
- **Gastronomy travellers.** One-to-two-day food-only stops in Antep, Hatay and Adana. Breakfast-crawl and market-day alignment, for example Fatih Çarşamba Pazarı on Wednesday.
- **Likya trekkers.** Stage planner (km per day, pansiyon endpoints, water), a season gate, and a rest-day buffer.

## 7. Accessibility, halal/alcohol, vegetarian, kids

### Takeaway
- **Vegetarian:** zeytinyağlı dishes and meze make vegetarian travel easy. Watch for et suyu (meat stock) in soups and pilav. Vegan is harder because dairy is everywhere.
- **Halal:** halal is the default for meat in Turkey; no source was retrieved on certification.
- **Alcohol:** retail sale stops at 22:00 nationwide, and availability can vary regionally during Ramadan.
- **Kids:** children under 8 are free at Efes and Ayasofya. Under-18 citizens have a free Müzekart Mobil.
- **Accessibility:** wheelchair access data was not found, and the planner should treat it as unknown per site.

### Cited Findings
- **Vegetarian:**
  - "Zeytinyağlı" dishes are olive-oil-based, served cold and usually meat-free; the hot dolma version often contains meat.
  - Vegans should expect back-and-forth over dairy. Use "vejetaryen" and ask "et suyu yok, değil mi?" because some vegetable soups and pilav use meat stock.
  - Four or five cold vegetable meze make a full meal at a meyhane.
  - Sources: [TurkeyTravelPlanner, vegetarians](https://turkeytravelplanner.com/details/Food/vegetarians.html); [istanbul.com, Istanbul for vegetarians](https://istanbul.com/blog/restaurant-bars/istanbul-for-vegetarians); [The Guide Istanbul](https://www.theguideistanbul.com/article/healthy-restaurants-istanbul/)
- **Alcohol:** the 22:00–06:00 retail ban covers tourism areas too. — [Yeni Birlik](https://www.gazetebirlik.com/yasam/tekel-bayileri-kacta-kacta-kapaniyor-2026-tekel-calisma-saatleri-ve-alkol-satis-saatleri/955343)
- **Ramadan alcohol:** availability may be restricted in some regions; in tourist districts licensed venues serve as usual. — [MyTurkeyAdventure](https://myturkeyadventure.com/ramadan-in-turkey); [Chasing the Donkey](https://www.chasingthedonkey.com/istanbul-in-ramadan-faqs-turkiye/)
- **Kids:**
  - Efes and Ayasofya are free for children under 8. — [Tours Around Turkey](https://toursaroundturkey.com/ephesus-entry-ticket-prices-update/); [istanbul.com TR](https://istanbul.com/tr/blog/iconic-structures/hagia-sophia-tickets-tours)
  - Müzekart Mobil is free for citizens aged 18 and under. — [KTB PDF](https://www.ktb.gov.tr/Eklenti/123238,muzekartpdfpdf.pdf); [Karekod](https://www.karekod.org/blog/muze-kart-fiyatlari/)
- **Accessibility:**
  - An "Engelsiz Müzekart" is listed as free. — [Karekod](https://www.karekod.org/blog/muze-kart-fiyatlari/)
  - Many sites involve walking or climbing, for example about 1 km uphill to the Nemrut terraces and 2–3 hours of outdoor walking at Göbeklitepe. — [NTV, Nemrut](https://www.ntv.com.tr/seyahat/galeri-nemrut-dagi-sezonun-son-turistlerini-agirliyor,wDpEWKHCIUGg0_Msm_Ldjw); [Pegasus blog](https://www.flypgs.com/blog/?p=8085)
- **Mosque etiquette:** remove shoes, women cover their heads, and avoid the five prayer times and Friday noon. — [istanbul.com TR, Ayasofya](https://istanbul.com/tr/blog/iconic-structures/hagia-sophia-tickets-tours)

### Inferences
- **Per-venue attributes.** Add `veg_friendly`, `vegan_options`, `alcohol_served` (licensed), `family_friendly`, `stroller_ok`, `step_free_access` (default "unknown"), `walk_km` and `elevation_gain`. Let users report these.
- **Alcohol-free travellers.** For halal-strict or alcohol-free personas, filter out meyhane and wine routes and substitute çay bahçesi, tatlıcı, kahvehane or iftar experiences.
- **Families with children.** Prefer short-walk sites, cap outdoor GAP sites in summer at mornings, insert midday rests, and use free under-8 entry.

  - wheelchair accessibility of major sites (Topkapı, Efes, Göreme, Pamukkale travertines) or of transport (YHT, Marmaray, ferries);
  - halal certification bodies and practices;
  - child discounts on balloons or tours;
  - baby-changing or stroller accessibility.

  Each of these needs a dedicated pass, for example using KTB "Engelsiz Müze" documentation and İBB accessibility data.
